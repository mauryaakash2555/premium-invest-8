# BM Wealth - Brand Guidelines

**DO NOT CHANGE without explicit approval from Akash Maurya**

### Background Colors
- **Deep Black:** `#000000` (Main background)

### Accent Colors
- **Pearl White:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary text)
- **Platinum:** `#E5E5E5` (Secondary text)

### Special Colors
- **WhatsApp Green:** `#25D366` (WhatsApp links only)

## Color Usage Guidelines

### Headers & CTAs
- Use Gold (#DAA520) for all headings
- Use Champagne Gold (#C0A062) for subheadings and accents

### Backgrounds
- Primary: Deep Black (#000000)
- Gradients: Black (#000000)
- Glass effects: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)

### Text
- Primary: Pearl White (#FFFFFF)
- Secondary: Platinum (#E5E5E5) or Champagne Gold (#C0A062)
- Disabled: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)

### Borders & Dividers
- Primary: rgba(218, 165, 32, 0.3) (Gold with transparency)
- Secondary: rgba(192, 160, 98, 0.2) (Champagne Gold with transparency)

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** 'Playfair Display' (serif) - For headings
- **Secondary Font:** 'Inter' (sans-serif) - For body text

## Design Principles
1. **Premium & Professional:** Maintain luxury aesthetic with gold accents
2. **High Contrast:** Ensure readability with white text on black backgrounds
3. **Consistency:** Use color palette consistently across all pages
4. **Accessibility:** Maintain WCAG AA standards for contrast ratios
