**BM WEALTH IMPACT ANALYSIS**

This isn't an isolated incident. It's a persistent issue in property registration, backed by official data.

💰 **The Real Numbers:**

**Official costs (₹2.3 crore property - Maharashtra, 2026 rates):**
- Stamp duty (6% for male buyer, including metro cess): ₹13,80,000
- Registration: ₹30,000 (capped for properties above ₹30 lakh)
- **Total legal: ₹14,10,000**

**Actual out-of-pocket (in this case):**
- Legal charges: ₹14,10,000
- "Facilitation" demand: ₹1,80,000 → negotiated to ₹1,20,000
- **Illegal markup: ~8.5% of legal fees**

📊 **Scale of the Problem (2025 ACB Data):**

Maharashtra Anti-Corruption Bureau registered:
- **682 corruption cases in 2025** (including 669 trap cases)
- **168 cases in Revenue & Land Records** (highest category)
- **Mumbai alone: 39 trap cases** (officially caught)
- Total bribe money recovered: **₹3.54 crores** across 559 cases
- **Average bribe: ₹63,326**

Reality check: These are only the reported and caught cases. Unofficial estimates suggest the actual scale could be much larger, potentially affecting thousands of registrations annually.

⚖️ **What The Law Says (vs Reality):**

**Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988:**
- Bribe demand: 3-7 years imprisonment
- Bribe-taking public servant: 6 months to 7 years

**Reality from ACB data:**
- 205 officials booked in corruption
- **Many not suspended from service**
- Conviction rates remain low

The system often sees delays, but enforcement varies.

🔍 **How It Can Work (Based on Reports):**

From verified accounts and news reports (2025-2026):

**Typical Process:**
- Submit documents online (portal often works)
- Office visit: Potential artificial delays raised
- Common issues: "Missing clarifications" or "additional NOCs"
- Unofficial contact: Amount discussed (scaled to property value)
  - Under ₹1 crore: ₹30,000-60,000
  - ₹1-2 crore: ₹80,000-1,20,000
  - ₹2-3 crore: ₹1,20,000-2,00,000
  - Above ₹3 crore: ₹2,00,000+
- Payment leads to faster processing

📉 **The Leverage Factor:**

Why some pay (from surveys and reports):
- EMI pressure
- Housing transitions
- Fear of prolonged delays
- Perception of normality

This can feel like added pressure, though not everyone encounters it.

💡 **What Could Help:**

**1. Enhanced Online Processes** (reduce human intervention)
	- Models from other states like Gujarat show promise
	- Maharashtra has made progress but room for improvement

**2. Strict Timelines**
	- Statutory: Aim for 15-30 days
	- Penalties for delays

**3. Digital Tracking**
	- Transparent audit trails

**4. Safe Reporting**
	- Protected whistleblower channels

📱 **How to Report (Realistically):**

**Official channels:**
- Maharashtra ACB: 1064
- acb.helpline@maharashtra.gov.in
- Expect follow-up; gather evidence like call records

**Better option:**
- Document interactions
- Seek media or legal support
